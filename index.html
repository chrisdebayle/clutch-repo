The Baseline

No formal quota and no prior-period actual. The baseline is capacity, territory, and strategy.
Why there's no target to hit yet
No formal quota. Sales leadership reviews self-set targets quarterly; ramp expectation is informal, tied to book-of-business growth over the first two years.MANAGER · SCOPING CALL

VENDOR DATA

How signal actually gets made
TECH EDGE
Best-in-class data intelligence, not guesswork

Origami and White Whale drive account and contact intelligence, backed by a disciplined dialer cadence run personally to keep outreach volume consistent — a 'whatever it takes' approach to the technology edge.

OEM ALIGNMENT
Strategized, not transactional, with OEM AEs

OEM account executives are engaged as active partners on my accounts, not treated as a rebate or spec resource. Success to validate in-seat: deal registration, MDF/co-op access, dedicated OEM SE time, and joint pipeline reviews — not just a strategic label.

FACE TIME
In the room with the actual buyer

Regional conferences and industry events put me in front of the CIOs, CTOs, and VPs of IT who own these decisions across Dallas/Ft. Worth, ahead of any cold outreach.

PATTERN DISRUPTION
Earned access, not another vendor lunch

High-touch relationship-building — sponsored socials and other shared experiences — builds top-of-mind share with key accounts that a generic outbound cadence can't.

This runs alongside the systemized rail above, not instead of it — Signal starts as often at a conference table as it does on a cold call.
What decides bid or no-bid
INCUMBENCY READ
Displacement fight, or a clean bid

A named incumbent with an active relationship means this is priced and pursued as a displacement fight, targeting the 15-25% band. No real incumbent, or a genuine multi-bidder RFP, means it's pursued as greenfield, targeting 50-60%.

PRICE-TO-WIN VS. MARGIN FLOOR
What it actually costs to win this

Estimate the price needed to beat the likely competitor and check it against the GP floor for this deal size. If winning requires pricing below the floor, this is a no-bid or a price-for-margin play, not a chase.

PURSUIT COST
What this costs to chase, win or lose

A five-minute requote and a full OEM sub-quote with dedicated SE time are not the same bet. Full pursuit effort is reserved for opportunities that clear the incumbency and margin checks first.

BID GATE
Opens only once incumbency is read, price-to-win clears the margin floor, and pursuit cost is justified by the deal's win-rate bucket. Everything else stays a no-bid, or gets a lighter-touch pursuit.
This is what keeps win rate calibrated, not just measured after the fact — if actuals drift outside either band tracked in KPI & Risk, the gate's criteria get adjusted before the pricing does.
